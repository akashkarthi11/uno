    A backup keeps a copy of a room (players, scores, settings and any game in progress) until you remove it — it never expires. Restoring puts the room back exactly as it was when saved; players come back when their page next checks in.

    Game rules

    • Before a game starts, the admin can tick which super cards are in the deck in the lobby; unticked ones are left out.
    • ★ Super cards (golden border): 🔁 Play Again (matches its colour — you go again), 🧹 Sweep (matches its colour — every other card of that colour in your hand goes down with it),💥 Blast (play any time, pick a colour — everyone else draws 1), 🔄 Swap Hands (play any time, pick a colour and a player — you trade hands), 🎁 Gift (play any time, pick a colour — give 1 random card to every other player, you always keep at least 1). The single plain ✨ Master card (play any time): pick a colour, then a player, then one super rule to apply to that player — 🔁 they play next, 🧹 they put down all cards of the new colour, 💥 they draw 2, 🔄 you swap hands with them, 🎁 you give them 2 cards, +2 / +4 they draw 2 / 4 cards and lose their next turn. You may pick yourself for 🔁 or 🧹.
    • 2–8 players. 123-card deck (108 classic + 15 super cards). The admin chooses how many cards each player gets (3–10, the official 7 by default), which table theme is used and which super cards are in the deck; the admin also starts the game. People who join mid-game play from the next one.
    • Play a card that matches the top card by colour, number or symbol. Wilds can be played any time, except Wild Draw Four, which needs a hand with no card of the current colour.
    • Skip skips the next player. Reverse flips direction (acts as Skip with 2 players). Draw Two / Wild Draw Four make the next player draw 2 / 4 and lose the turn.
    • No playable card? Click the draw pile. If the drawn card fits you may play it, otherwise press Pass.
    • Turn timer (30 seconds unless the admin changed it in the lobby): if you haven't picked up or played a card in time, you draw 1 extra card and the turn moves to the next player.
    • GrowX! button: when you hold 2 cards, press GrowX! before you play your second-to-last card. If you play it without pressing, anyone can tap 🎯 Catch! before the next player moves and you draw 2 cards. Pressing GrowX! right after playing still saves you.
    • Stacking (if the admin switched it on): a +2 can be answered with another +2, a +4 with another +4. The total goes to the first player who cannot stack.
    • Challenge +4 / +2 (on unless the admin switched it off): when a Wild Draw Four or a Draw Two is played, the next player gets a question: "Does your opponent have a red card?" (the colour that was in play before the card). Yes: if they really do hold that colour the challenge succeeds and they draw the cards instead; if not, you draw the 4 or 2 and lose your turn. No or Skip challenge: you draw the cards and lose your turn as usual. The result is shown for a few seconds.
    • Scoring (official): the first player out scores the value of every card left in the others' hands: numbers face value, Skip / Reverse / Draw Two 20, Wild / Wild Draw Four 50 (our own cards: 20 colour ones, 50 wild ones). Points add up over the games; the first to 500 wins the match and a new match starts. Play still goes on after the first player is out, and the last player holding cards loses.
    • The first card turned over is never a Wild. If it is a Skip the first player is skipped, a Reverse turns play the other way, a Draw Two makes the first player draw 2. Everyone is dealt 7 cards unless the admin chooses another number (3–10).
    • If a player's connection drops, the game pauses and the admin decides: wait, remove them, or restart. 🚪 Leave takes you out of the game; you can sign in again to join as a new player.
    • 🔔 Alerts shows a notification when it is your turn while this page is in the background (the page must stay open). 📊 Stats shows this room's wins and losses and its recent games.
    • Losing: play continues after someone empties their hand — they are out and ranked. The last player still holding cards loses.
