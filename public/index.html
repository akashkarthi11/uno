    Game rules

    • 2–8 players. 108-card deck, 5 cards each. The admin starts the game; people who join mid-game play from the next one.
    • Play a card that matches the top card by colour, number or symbol. Wilds can be played any time, except Wild Draw Four, which needs a hand with no card of the current colour.
    • Skip skips the next player. Reverse flips direction (acts as Skip with 2 players). Draw Two / Wild Draw Four make the next player draw 2 / 4 and lose the turn.
    • No playable card? Click the draw pile. If the drawn card fits you may play it, otherwise press Pass.
    • GrowX call: press GrowX! when you have 2 cards or fewer. If you reach one card without calling it, the next action by anyone — or a click on Catch! — makes you draw 2.
    • If a player's connection drops, their turns are played automatically; they can rejoin by reopening the link.
    • Losing: play continues after someone empties their hand — they are out and ranked. The last player still holding cards loses.
