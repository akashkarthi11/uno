    Game rules

    • Before a game starts, the admin can tick which super cards are in the deck in the lobby; unticked ones are left out.
    • ★ Super cards (golden border): 🔁 Play Again (matches its colour — you go again), 🧹 Sweep (matches its colour — every other card of that colour in your hand goes down with it),💥 Blast (play any time, pick a colour — everyone else draws 1), 🔄 Swap Hands (play any time, pick a colour and a player — you trade hands), 🎁 Gift (play any time, pick a colour — give 1 random card to every other player, you always keep at least 1). The single plain ✨ Master card (play any time): pick a colour, then a player, then one super rule to apply to that player — 🔁 they play next, 🧹 they put down all cards of the new colour, 💥 they draw 2, 🔄 you swap hands with them, 🎁 you give them 2 cards, +2 / +4 they draw 2 / 4 cards and lose their next turn. You may pick yourself for 🔁 or 🧹.
    • 2–8 players. 123-card deck (108 classic + 15 super cards), 5 cards each.
      • The admin starts the game; people who join mid-game play from the next one.
    • Play a card that matches the top card by colour, number or symbol. Wilds can be played any time, except Wild Draw Four, which needs a hand with no card of the current colour.
    • Skip skips the next player. Reverse flips direction (acts as Skip with 2 players). Draw Two / Wild Draw Four make the next player draw 2 / 4 and lose the turn.
    • No playable card? Click the draw pile. If the drawn card fits you may play it, otherwise press Pass.
    • 30-second turn: if you haven't picked up or played a card in 30 seconds, you draw 1 extra card and the turn moves to the next player.
    • GrowX! button: when you hold 2 cards, press GrowX! before you play your second-to-last card. If you play it without pressing, you draw 1 extra card.
    • If a player's connection drops, their turns are played automatically; they can rejoin by reopening the link.
    • Losing: play continues after someone empties their hand — they are out and ranked. The last player still holding cards loses.
